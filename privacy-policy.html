Claim Pulse Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026 · F4 Software LLC

The short version: Claim Pulse reads your VA claim information from VA.gov using your own sign-in, keeps it only in your browser on your computer, and never sends it to us or anyone else. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no selling data.

Who we are

Claim Pulse is a browser extension made by F4 Software LLC. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs.

What information Claim Pulse uses

When you are signed in to VA.gov, Claim Pulse requests your claim, decision review, and appeal status from VA's own API at api.va.gov, the same information VA.gov loads when you check your claim status. Depending on your account, this can include:

  • Claim types, dates, status and processing steps
  • Conditions you claimed and issues under review (this can be health information)
  • Names of documents VA has received and requests VA has made
  • Decision review and appeal status, decisions, and deadlines
  • Names of people listed on certain applications, if VA includes them

Claim Pulse never sees or stores your VA.gov username, password, or sign-in codes. Your browser handles your VA.gov sign-in; the extension only asks VA's API for your claim status while that sign-in is active.

Where it is stored

Everything Claim Pulse saves is kept in your browser's local extension storage on your computer. It is used only to show your claims, estimate timelines, and tell you what changed since your last check.

What we share

Nothing. Claim Pulse does not send your information to F4 Software or to any other company or server. The only website it contacts is api.va.gov, to get your own claim status. It contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking code, and it does not load code from the internet.

How Claim Pulse uses VA.gov pages

When you open your claim status or My VA pages on VA.gov, Claim Pulse notices the visit so it can refresh your saved status. It does not read or record the contents of those pages or any other website you visit.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

Your saved information stays on your computer until you delete it. You can erase it at any time from Claim Pulse's Settings ("Erase saved claim info from this computer"), or by removing the extension, which deletes all of its data.

Limited use

Claim Pulse's use of your information is limited to providing the features described in its Chrome Web Store listing and user interface. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, use it to decide creditworthiness or lending, or transfer it to anyone.

Children

Claim Pulse is intended for veterans and their families and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
